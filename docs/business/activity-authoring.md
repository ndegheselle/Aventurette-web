## The Créer tab (B05)

Three entries, and only those:

- *Créer un événement*: dates, places, times, nights, groups. It continues in the
  [planning](planning.md#an-event).
- *Créer une activité*: a blank sheet with the common structure, or a copy of an existing
  activity to adapt.
- *Créer une journée type*: it continues in the [planning](planning.md#day-templates).

Creating happens here; finding something already made happens elsewhere: day templates in the
planning, personal content in [Mes contenus](account.md#my-contents).

## The form (A19, A04)

Every activity has the same structure, so the form is the same for a new sheet and for an
edited one:

- the name;
- the format: *Petit jeu*, *Grand jeu*, *Atelier*, *Sortie*;
- the duration and the number of players;
- the pedagogical objectives;
- the imaginary world and the theme;
- the preparation;
- the steps, with their instructions and resources.

*Annuler* drops the changes, *Enregistrer* keeps them.

## Rules

- **A copy keeps its source, and never changes it.** *La source éventuelle est conservée ; cette
  activité est votre copie.*
- **A shared activity is not edited directly.** *Modifier* on it makes the user's own copy.
- A new activity starts as a personal draft, and shows in
  [*Mes créations*](catalogue.md#mes-créations).
- **Creating an activity is a permission**, given per structure and role
  ([Autorisations](account.md#settings)).

## Not decided

- Whether a personal activity can be offered to the structure, or to everyone.
- Whether a copy hears of later changes to its source. The design implies it does not.
