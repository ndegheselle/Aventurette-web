# Activity sheet

One sheet per activity, the same wherever it is opened from: the catalogue, the favourites, the
planning, the quick mode. It opens full screen, without the tabs.

| Screen | | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A03 | The sheet | Built |
| A05 | *Préparation*: material, setup, advice | Built |

## The sheet (A03)

At the top:

- the title, then the format, the pace and the duration: *Grand jeu · Dynamique · 1 h 30*;
- *Extérieur* / *Intérieur*, when the activity has both variants: the choice adapts the steps and
  the material. An activity with one variant shows it alone;
- the ages and the number of players: *8–11 ans / 11–14 ans · 8–20 joueurs*;
- the leaders it needs and how much it asks of them: *2 animateurs · mobilisation élevée*;
- the objectives: *Coopération et communication*;
- the imaginary world, as a family and a sub-family, *Aventure › Pirates*, and the theme apart
  from it, *Océan*.

Then three sections, each opening a page:

- *Préparation et matériel* — what to prepare, and how long it takes (A05);
- *Déroulé* — the steps: explain, launch, conclude;
- *Conseils, sécurité et variantes*.

When the activity has safety instructions, a warning shows with their count, and opens them in
full.

At the bottom, four actions:

- *Partager* — [share, export or print](sharing.md);
- *Enregistrer* — the [favourite](catalogue.md#favourites);
- *Modifier* — [edit it](activity-authoring.md). On a sheet the user does not own, this makes
  their own copy;
- *Mode guidé* — [run it](running-an-activity.md). The only way into a run.

There is no *Dupliquer*: adapting an activity goes through *Créer*.

## What an activity is made of

The content follows the activity sheet template that Ajil writes in the back office.

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| Identity | Name, a summary of 300 characters at most shown under the title, a description, a picture, a state (draft or published). |
| Classification | The format (*Petit jeu*, *Grand jeu*, *Atelier*, *Sortie*), the themes. |
| Imaginary world | The universes, each a family and a sub-family. |
| Audience | An age range, a range of participants. |
| Supervision | How many leaders it needs. |
| Place | Indoor or outdoor, and the places it suits: park, house, forest, lake… |
| Safety | Safety instructions, picked from a shared list, each with its precautions. |
| Pedagogy | Goals, what it is ideal for, the development axes it works on. |
| Steps | In order. Each has a kind (preparing the game, a free step, or the conclusion), a title, an estimated duration, a description, the material and resources it uses, and its tips. |
| Material | Picked from a shared list, each with a quantity in words: *un par équipe* is a quantity too. |
| Workshops | Stations run at the same time: a name, a theme, challenges, the material each uses, the adults each needs. |
| Resources | Files, pictures and documents, attached to the steps. |

What the sheet derives from them:

- **Preparation time** is the sum of the steps that prepare the game; **play time** is the sum of
  the others. A step with no duration counts as zero.
- **The activity's resources** are those of its steps, each listed once.
- **A range with no bound** on one side has no limit on that side.

## Préparation (A05)

Three tabs: *Matériel*, *Installation*, *Conseils*.

- *Matériel*: each item with its quantity and a note (*20 cartes indices — à imprimer et
  découper*).
- *Installation*: what to set up, and how long it takes (*15 min*).
- *Conseils*: safety (*vérifier les limites et les zones interdites*), and the other variant
  (*réduire la zone et adapter les déplacements*).
- **Ticking material is a checklist for the user, nothing more**: it changes no stock and is
  saved nowhere.
- At the bottom, *Retour à la fiche*.

When the activity is placed in an event, this material and preparation become the event's
[tasks](preparation.md).

## Differs from the design

- **The steps, the material, the resources and the workshops are listed on the sheet itself**,
  rather than behind *Déroulé* and *Préparation et matériel*.
- **Rich text shows as plain paragraphs and bullets**, without bold or links.
- **Pictures are placeholders.**
- ***Partager* and *Modifier* only say they are not available yet.**
- **In *Préparation*:**
  - a material shows its name, and its quantity as the line under it: an activity's material has
    no note of its own;
  - a workshop's material is listed apart, under the workshop's name, and the files the steps use
    under *Ressources*;
  - *Installation* is the steps that prepare the game, each with its duration;
  - *Conseils* is the safety instructions and the tips of every step. The other variant's advice
    (*Variante intérieure*) is not there: an activity does not describe its variants;
  - the ticks last until the app is closed.
