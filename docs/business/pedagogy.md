Two segments: *Fils conducteurs* and *Ressources*.

## Fils conducteurs

A fil conducteur is the pedagogical thread of a period, for a group: *Donner du sens à l'année*.

**The list** (D02): a search by name, group or period; *En préparation*, *En cours*, *Terminés*;
each with its group, its period, and how many events are linked to it. *+ Créer un fil
conducteur*.

**Filling one in** goes through five parts, each saved before moving to the next:

1. **Contexte** (D03): its name, the period, the group or unit, and the needs the team noticed —
   *Autonomie*, *Cohésion*, *Expression*, *Confiance* — with details.
2. **Objectifs pédagogiques** (D04): the axes to develop — *Autonomie*, *Coopération*,
   *Créativité*, *Citoyenneté* — the objectives to tick (*Prendre sa place dans une équipe*),
   and how to observe them.
3. **Thèmes à explorer** (D05): a search, families (*Nature*, *Vivre ensemble*, *Arts*,
   *Sciences*, *Aventure*), the themes to tick, and the user's own.
4. **Imaginaire / fil rouge** (D06): the world — *Aventure*, *Fantastique*, *Enquête*, or *Sans
   imaginaire* — its sub-family (*Aventure → Pirates*), the collective mission, the characters and
   roles, how the story moves on (*un indice nouveau à chaque sortie*).
5. **Fil blanc** (D07), **optional**: a spiritual intention. It depends on the structure, and can
   stay off. *Émerveillement*, *Gratitude*, *Intériorité*, *Solidarité*, the intention in words,
   and the moments for it.

**The summary** (D08) shows each part, and the linked events. *Relier des événements* links more.

An [event](planning.md#an-event) shows its fil conducteur; a [report](follow-up.md#the-report)
can cover one.

## Resources

- **Ajil's** (D09): guides, ideas and pedagogical landmarks, written by Ajil. A search, and
  filters by age, objective and format. Each says its kind and reading time: *Conseil Ajil · 5 min
  de lecture*.
- **The structure's** (D11): its own documents and advice, **read-only, and shown according to
  the structure's permissions**.
- **A resource** (D10): its title, kind, tags (*Coopération*, *11–14 ans*), its text, and *Mettre
  en pratique*, which lists the activities that go with it. *Partager*, *Enregistrer*.
