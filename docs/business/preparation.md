# Preparation and inventory

The tasks an event needs before it happens, who does them, and the material they use. None of it
is built.

| Screen | | Status |
|---|---|---|
| B15 | An event's *Préparation* | Not built |
| B19 | *Attribuer une tâche* | Not built |
| C02 | *Ma tâche* | Not built |
| B20 | *Inventaire* of the structure | Not built |

## Where tasks come from

- **From the activities of an event**: their material and preparation, as the
  [sheet](activity-sheet.md#préparation-a05) lists them. *Imprimer 20 cartes indices*,
  *Préparer les 4 foulards*.
- **From its temps de vie**: their usual tasks. *Installer le coin calme*.
- **Added by hand.**

A task always belongs to an event, and to the block it came from. It is not a module of its own:
*Mes tâches* is only a filter.

## Giving out tasks

In the event's *Préparation* (B15), **and only there** (UF-04):

- the tasks, as *Toutes*, *À faire* or *Prêtes*; each with its title, where it comes from
  (*La quête du remède · Matériel*, *Temps libre · tâche habituelle*), its responsable and its due
  date;
- *Attribuer une tâche* (B19): the event, the responsable, the due date, instructions.

Rules:

- **Tasks are assigned and reassigned here only.** In [Suivi](follow-up.md#à-faire), everyone sees
  their own tasks and nothing else.
- **Changing the responsable does not reset *Prêt*.**
- Only the people concerned by a change are told.

## Doing a task

*Ma tâche* (C02):

- *Attribuée à moi · avant le 21 novembre*;
- ***À faire* or *Prêt*, and the user can go back from *Prêt***;
- a link to the event, and to the sheet the task comes from;
- a checklist drawn from the sheet's material and preparation;
- *Besoin d'aide* sends a request to the people concerned; *Marquer prêt*.

To have a task given to someone else, the responsable changes it in the planning.

## Material and stock

From UF-06:

- **An event's needs are added up**: per activity, and in total, with a list to tick.
- The inventory (B20) shows, for each item, what is available against what is needed:
  *Disponible : 8 · besoin : 4*. Material to print shows how many copies: *À imprimer : 20
  exemplaires*.
- **Ticking a task never lowers the stock.** A movement is confirmed by hand: a purchase (the
  quantity received), a return, a loss, a consumption.
- The inventory belongs to the structure, and is reached from
  [*Structures*](account.md#settings).
- The material is reached from the event and from each planned activity. **In the guided mode,
  only the current step's material shows.**
- The event's [folder](sharing.md#a-folder-to-export-or-print-a11-a12) can include the material and the
  resources to print.
