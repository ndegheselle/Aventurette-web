Three segments: *À faire*, *Bilans*, *Historique*.

## À faire

Everything waiting on the user (C01), in three groups:

- *Mes tâches* — their own [tasks](preparation.md), with the event and the due date;
- *Validations* — *2 validations en attente*, shown only to the people allowed to validate;
- *Bilans à faire* — one per saved [réalisation](running-an-activity.md#finishing).

## Validations

From UF-01 and UF-03:

- **Validation is optional.** A structure's *direction pédagogique* switches it on. Who may
  validate is set per structure and event.
- **Without it**, whoever is allowed adds activities directly, and no status shows.
- **With it**, a request is made for one activity or a whole event, and the people allowed to
  validate are told.
- **Validating covers the objectives, the programme, the organisation, the responsibilities and
  the material. It does not wait for the preparation tasks**, which stay independent.
- The reviewer validates activity by activity, or the whole event (C03).
- ***À ajuster* sends a comment to the creator** (C08), who changes the activity and sends it
  again. The other activities stay validated.
- **There is no *Refusée*.** A request can be withdrawn.
- **Validating a whole event that still has items *À ajuster* asks the reviewer to confirm**
  they checked the changes (C09).
- Each activity's status shows in the [planning](planning.md): *À valider*, *À ajuster*,
  *Validée*.

## Bilans

From UF-11:

- **Three kinds**: a run's bilan (one réalisation), an event's or a day's (a global one at the
  end of the day), and a period's (a term, a half-year, the end of a camp — how often depends on
  the structure).
- The list (C04): *À compléter* or *Terminés*, a search by activity, event or period, and
  *Partager une synthèse*.
- A bilan (C06): participation and how the children felt; the objectives, *Atteints*, *En
  partie* or *Non atteints*; observations and adaptations; points to watch; what to pass on.
- **It tells apart what the children enjoyed and which objectives were reached.**
- **A bilan belongs to its réalisation.** Changing it never adds a second entry to the history.
- What is to be passed on goes to the responsables allowed to read it, in one grouped
  notification.

## History

- The réalisations saved (C05), over six months, one year, two years or a chosen period, for a
  group; how they split by category (*Sport et mouvement · 18*, *Ateliers manuels · 12*…).
- **An entry opens its réalisation and its bilan. There is no *Rejouer*.**
- *Préparer un rapport* leads to the report.

## The report

*Rapport pédagogique* (C07): a period, a group or a [fil conducteur](pedagogy.md), and its
sections:

- the activities run, and how they split by category;
- the objectives, with the team's observations;
- how practice changed: rhythms, durations, diversity;
- the detailed bilans, as far as the user is allowed to see them.

*Prévisualiser*, *Enregistrer*, *Partager*. **The trends are the group's. The report never
measures a child's own progress.**
