# Sharing and exporting

*Partager* opens a panel, not a page. It applies to an activity, an event, a day template, a
pedagogical resource and a report. None of it is built: *Partager* only says it is not available
yet.

What others shared with the user is listed in the catalogue's

## The panel (A10)

Three choices:

- *Partager un lien* — read-only access;
- *Exporter en PDF* — everything, or chosen sections;
- *Imprimer* — the same folder, for a printer.

## A folder to export or print (A11, A12)

- The format: PDF or print.
- The scope (*Événement entier ▾*), the period (*Du 21 au 22 novembre 2026*), the groups.
- The sections to include, each ticked or not:
  - *Planning et horaires* — the day or period chosen;
  - *Fiches d'activités* — the full steps of each game;
  - *Matériel consolidé* — the quantities, and a list to tick;
  - *Préparation et responsabilités* — the tasks and the resources;
  - *Bilans pédagogiques* — only when the user is allowed to see them.
- *Aperçu* shows the folder before it is made. **The PDF and the print use the same sections and
  the same period.**

## A link (A13)

- To chosen people, or to the user's team.
- **Read-only.** The recipients see the source; they cannot change the sheet.
- The right given is *Consultation*.
- **The link follows the permissions, and copies nothing.** A recipient who wants to adapt the
  content [makes their own copy](activity-authoring.md#rules).
